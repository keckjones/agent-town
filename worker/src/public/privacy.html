Privacy Policy

KJ Agentic · College Station, Texas · Last updated October 6, 2026

KJ Agentic is a small local business that builds websites and runs online shops. This policy explains what information our internal business software ("KJ Agentic Command Center") handles, and why.

Who uses this software

The Command Center is a private tool used only by the owner of KJ Agentic. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

Google account access

The owner connects the business Gmail account (agentickj@gmail.com) so the software can send business emails the owner has approved. When connecting, the software asks Google for:

  • Send email on your behalf (gmail.send): used only to send emails the owner has reviewed and approved, such as replies to customers and outreach to local businesses. The software cannot read, delete or change any email through this permission.
  • Your email address (userinfo.email): used only to confirm that the business account, not another account, was connected.

Google sign-in tokens are stored encrypted at rest in a private database table that only the business's own server can read. They are never shown in the dashboard, shared, or sold. KJ Agentic's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Information about businesses and customers

  • Businesses we contact: public information such as business name, address, phone number, public website and a public contact email, with a note of where each fact was found.
  • Customers and people who write to us: their name, email address and the messages they send, so we can reply and provide the service they asked for.
  • Payments and orders: handled by our payment and store providers (such as Stripe, Etsy and Shopify). We receive order and payment confirmations; we never see or store full card numbers.

How we use it

  • To contact businesses about our services, answer messages, deliver work, and keep business records.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not use it for advertising profiles.
  • Some text is processed by AI service providers (such as Anthropic) to help draft messages and summaries for the owner to review. Those providers process it on our behalf only.

Opting out

If you receive an email from us and don't want more, reply "unsubscribe" or email agentickj@gmail.com. You'll be added to our do-not-contact list permanently.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep business records as long as needed to run the business and meet legal obligations. To ask us to delete information about you, email agentickj@gmail.com.

The owner can disconnect the Gmail account at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections. The software then stops sending immediately.
